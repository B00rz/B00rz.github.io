AnimeForge privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

AnimeForge is a personal tool used by one person to upload recap videos to their own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to the data Google handles.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, AnimeForge is allowed to upload videos to that channel, set their thumbnails, change their privacy status and publish time, and read the channel's name. It doesn't read comments, subscribers, viewers or any other account's data.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is stored on the owner's own computers so uploads keep working. AnimeForge also keeps a record of each video it uploaded (its YouTube id, link, title and scheduled date). Nothing else from the Google account is stored.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. It is only sent to YouTube, to make the uploads.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access). Deleting the stored token on the owner's computers removes AnimeForge's copy.
